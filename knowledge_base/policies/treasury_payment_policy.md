# Treasury Payment Policy and Delegation of Authority

## Payment runs
- Regular payment runs are made every Tuesday and Friday from the operating account.
- Payments outside a run are allowed only for MSME deadlines, early-payment discounts and statutory dues.
- Every payment must carry the invoice number and a payment reference in the bank narration.

## Delegation of authority (who can approve what)
- Up to Rs 5,00,000 per vendor in any 7-day window: may be released automatically after all checks pass.
- Above Rs 5,00,000 per vendor (one invoice or several invoices added together within the window): Finance Controller approval is required.
- Above Rs 50,00,000: CFO approval plus Board-delegated authority, outside the ProcureGuard system.
- Splitting one supply into several invoices, or paying invoices one at a time to stay under a limit, is a control violation. The limit always applies to the total paid to the vendor in the window.

## Early-payment discounts
- Take a cash discount when the vendor is LOW risk, the invoice is fully matched and the cash buffer stays above the minimum after payment.
- Rule of thumb: "2/10 Net 30" means paying 20 days early to save 2%. That works out to roughly 36% a year (2% x 365 / 20), far above the company's cost of short-term funds, so it should almost always be taken.
- The discount is calculated on the taxable value (before GST). GST is paid in full.
- No early payments to MEDIUM or HIGH risk vendors.

## Cash buffer
- The operating account must keep at least the minimum cash buffer (MIN_CASH_BUFFER_INR in the P2P policy, currently Rs 20,00,000) after committed outflows for the next 7 days.
- If a payment would break the buffer, it is scheduled for the next run instead, unless it is an MSME deadline payment, in which case Treasury arranges funds from the cash credit line.

## Payment holds by risk
- HIGH risk vendors: all payments frozen until the Vendor Master team re-verifies the vendor.
- Vendors in a bank-change cooling period: frozen until the call-back is recorded (see the Vendor Master Change SOP).
- GSTIN cancelled or suspended: the taxable value may be paid only after the Finance Controller's review; the GST portion is withheld until the vendor shows that the registration is restored, because input tax credit is not available.
