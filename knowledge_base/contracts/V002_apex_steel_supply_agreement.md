# Supply Agreement - Apex Steel Traders (V002)

## Parties and term
- Supplier: Apex Steel Traders, GSTIN 07AAFFA9876K1Z2.
- Term: 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.
- Category: raw material, MS steel sheets.

## Price and price-variation clause
- MS steel sheet 2 mm (STL-MS-2MM): base price Rs 420.00 per sheet plus GST at 18%, as on the PO date.
- Price variation is allowed only once a quarter and only if the published steel price index for the previous month has moved by more than 5%.
- Any price change must be agreed in a written PO amendment BEFORE dispatch. A higher price printed on an invoice, or mentioned in an invoice note, is not a valid price change.
- Without a PO amendment, AP pays the PO price and raises a debit note for the difference.

## Quality, rejection and debit notes
- Sheets with rust, or thickness below 1.9 mm, are rejected at the plant.
- Rejected sheets are not payable. The supplier must collect them within 15 days at its own cost, and the company raises a debit note for any rejected quantity that was billed.

## Payment terms
- Net 45 days from acceptance of the goods.
